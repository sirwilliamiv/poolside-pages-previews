Questions

What if I don't like the preview?

Tell us what to change and we change it. If it's still not for you, the preview comes down and you owe nothing.

Do I lose my current site or domain?

No. Nothing changes until you say go. Then we point your existing domain at the new site; the old one stays wherever it is until you cancel it.

Who writes the words?

We draft them from what your current site says and from your Google listing. You review everything before it goes live, and we never invent claims about your business.

Can I edit the site myself?

Yes. It's a normal WordPress site on your own hosting, with your own login. Most owners just email us the change instead.

What trades do you work with?

Pool builders and pool service, screen enclosures, pavers and hardscape, outdoor kitchens and living, landscape lighting, fence, and decks. Anything with a gallery of finished work.

Is there a contract?

Monthly is month to month. The one-time build is one payment; the care plan after it can be cancelled any time.
