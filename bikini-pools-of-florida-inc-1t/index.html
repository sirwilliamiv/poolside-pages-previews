About Bikini Pools of Florida, Inc.

Bikini Pools of Florida has been servicing the Tampa Bay Area since 2004. Whether you need new equipment, a pool resurfacing, a complete remodel, or even a new wider paver driveway, Bikini Pools can help you create the backyard, or front yard, of your dreams.

Specializing in extreme pool makeovers, we are equipped to tackle a full scope of pool repair and renovation services. Our team is available by appointment only, ready to conduct an inspection and provide you with an honest estimate.
