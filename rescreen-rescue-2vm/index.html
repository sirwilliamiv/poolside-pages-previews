Questions we get every week

Why should I repair small tears in my screen promptly?

Addressing torn screens or structural damage quickly prevents further deterioration and helps keep your enclosure sturdy, functional, and bug-free.

When is it time to replace screen enclosure hardware?

Hardware should be replaced when old components become rusty, corroded, or damaged, ensuring the structure remains safe and secure.

What are the benefits of a full rescreen?

A full rescreen removes worn netting to protect your pool area from insects and debris without sacrificing your view of the outdoors.
