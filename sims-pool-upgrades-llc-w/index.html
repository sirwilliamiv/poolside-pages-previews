Questions we get every week

What is fiberglass pool resurfacing?

Fiberglass pool resurfacing restores the protective gelcoat and structural surface of your pool, addressing issues like fading, cracking, and peeling.

How do I know if my fiberglass pool needs resurfacing?

Signs include a faded or discolored finish, cracks, a rough surface, blistering or peeling gelcoat, chalky buildup, or persistent staining.

Why choose fiberglass specialists for my pool?

Companies that focus on fiberglass deliver better results because it is their core expertise, unlike those who offer it as an additional service.
